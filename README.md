The case study is based on a workforce-planning challenge I addressed for a financial institution. 

The organization’s ERP system captured historical customer-support activity, but it did not include a workforce forecasting (WFO). 

As a result, the operation was unable to generate reliable short-term and long-term forecasts or translate anticipated chat demand into staffing requirements.

To provide an immediate and reusable solution, I developed an Excel-based Erlang forecasting tool for the Chat line of business. 

The tool was designed as a plug-and-play solution that could convert historical demand and operational assumptions into actionable workforce requirements. 

==========================================================================================================================
It supports forecasting and capacity-planning inputs such as:

Forecasted chat volume and interval distribution
Average handling time and workload hours
Productive FTE requirements
Shrinkage and scheduling inefficiency
Total rostered FTE requirements
Short-term and long-term staffing scenarios
==========================================================================================================================

The solution allowed the institution to make more informed staffing and scheduling decisions while it did not have a dedicated workforce optimization platform.
